TASK (f) — Monthly Revenue Reconciliation

Objective:
Compare the monthly revenue calculated from the sales data with
finance_monthly.csv and identify the reason for every difference.

Revenue calculation:
Revenue includes SALE, RETURN, DISCOUNT and VOID lines.
TAX and TENDER lines are excluded because TAX is GST and TENDER
represents the amount paid by the customer.

Difference 1 — March 2024:

Our revenue is lower than Finance by 486,250.00.

Cause:
This is a difference in revenue scope. Finance includes an
institutional order of 486,250.00 that was invoiced outside the
till system. Therefore, the order is not present in the sales
folder used by the pipeline.

Evidence:
March bulk invoice = 486,250.00

41,971,649.09 + 486,250.00 = 42,457,899.09

Therefore the Finance figure is explained exactly.


Difference 2 — July 2024:

Our revenue is lower than Finance by 232,131.70.

Cause:
This is a source-data problem. Store S07 lost its till server
for three days in July 2024, so the corresponding sales exports
are missing from the source folder.

Missing files:
SALES_S07_20240709
SALES_S07_20240710
SALES_S07_20240711

The sales data contains only 28 distinct days for S07 in July,
while the other stores contain all 31 days.

Finance has the numbers for these missing days because the store
provided them separately.


Difference 3 — December 2024:

Our revenue is higher than Finance by 50.48.

Cause:
This is a difference in revenue calculation/rounding. Finance
rounds each bill to the nearest rupee before calculating the
monthly total, while the pipeline sums the transaction amounts
with paise precision.

The difference is only 50.48 and is explained by the different
rounding method.


October validation:

October revenue matches exactly:

Our revenue:     56,359,195.92
Finance revenue: 56,359,195.92

Therefore, the pipeline did not produce the approximately
double October revenue warned about in the question.

Conclusion:

Only March, July and December differ from the Finance figures.

March is a revenue-scope difference caused by an institutional
order outside the till.

July is a source-data problem caused by three missing S07 export
days.

December is a rounding/revenue-calculation difference.

The July source-data issue should be taken back to the Finance
team because the source folder is missing S07's three days while
Finance has the corresponding numbers.
