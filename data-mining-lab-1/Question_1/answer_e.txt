TASK (e) — Query Across the Two Systems

Objective: Query sales data stored in the MinIO object store together
with store and product information stored in PostgreSQL, without first
copying either side into the other.

Architecture: - MinIO stores the partitioned sales CSV files. -
PostgreSQL stores the stores and products master tables. - DuckDB acts
as the analytical query engine and accesses both systems.

Cross-system query: The following single DuckDB query reads sales
directly from MinIO and joins them with PostgreSQL tables:

SELECT st.store_id, st.store_name, p.product_code, COUNT() AS
sales_lines, ROUND(SUM(s.qty s.unit_price), 2) AS revenue FROM
read_csv_auto(
’s3://sales/partitioned_sales/store=S01/month=2024-01/*.csv’ ) s JOIN
pg.public.stores st ON st.store_id = SPLIT_PART(s.bill_no, ‘/’, 1) JOIN
pg.public.products p ON p.product_code = s.product_code WHERE
s.line_type = ‘SALE’ GROUP BY st.store_id, st.store_name, p.product_code
ORDER BY revenue DESC LIMIT 10;

The query successfully returned 10 rows for store S01 (Annapurna
Jayanagar), proving that DuckDB joined data from MinIO and PostgreSQL in
one query.

Execution evidence: EXPLAIN ANALYZE showed: - READ_CSV_AUTO reading the
MinIO S3 path. - Total Files Read: 31. - 9,260 rows read from the sales
files. - TABLE_SCAN of the PostgreSQL stores table (12 rows). -
TABLE_SCAN of the PostgreSQL products table (1,224 rows). - HASH_JOIN
operators joining the sales data with stores and products. - FILTER
applying line_type = ‘SALE’. - HASH_GROUP_BY performing COUNT and SUM
aggregation. - TOP_N performing the final revenue ordering and LIMIT
10. - HTTPFS statistics showed actual object-store activity: 41 GET
requests.

Therefore, the evidence from DuckDB’s actual execution plan shows that
the sales files were accessed through MinIO/HTTPFS, PostgreSQL tables
were scanned through the PostgreSQL connection, and the joins,
filtering, aggregation and ordering were executed by DuckDB.

Note: The demonstration query joins products using product_code only.
For historical reporting, the production design should additionally
match the transaction date to products.valid_from and products.valid_to,
because product codes may be reissued.
