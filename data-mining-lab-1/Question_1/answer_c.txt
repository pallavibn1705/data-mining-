TASK (c) — Dimensional Model for Dashboard

Objective:
Design tables that allow the dashboard to slice revenue by store,
product category, day of the week and month without repeating
descriptive information on every sales row.

Design:
A dimensional model was created using:

1. dim_store
2. dim_category
3. dim_product
4. dim_date
5. fact_sales

Dimension tables:

dim_store:
Stores store_id, store_name, address_line, city, state and region.
The store name and address are stored once instead of being repeated
for every sales transaction.

dim_category:
Stores category_id, category_name, department and GST rate.

dim_product:
Stores product_sk, product_code, product_name, category_id, brand,
pack size, unit of measure and product validity dates.

dim_date:
Stores the date, day of week, day name, day of month, month number,
month name, quarter and year.

Fact table:

fact_sales stores:
store_id, product_sk, category_id, sale_date, bill_no, line_no,
quantity, unit_price, line_type and revenue.

Revenue handling:

SALE lines contribute positive revenue.
RETURN lines reduce revenue.
DISCOUNT lines reduce revenue.
VOID lines are retained so that cancelled transactions are correctly
accounted for.
TAX and TENDER lines are excluded because they are not revenue.

Product-code reissue:

A product_code alone was not used to identify a product because retired
product codes can be reused. Instead, the transaction date was matched
against valid_from and valid_to to obtain the correct product_sk.

Validation:

Fact table rows loaded: 789,516
Rows with product: 766,796
Discount rows without product: 22,720
Total revenue: 522,865,735.75

The dimensional model was verified by successfully querying revenue by:
- Store
- Product category
- Day of week
- Month

Indexes were created on store_id, product_sk, category_id, sale_date
and line_type in fact_sales to support faster dashboard queries.

Conclusion:

The dimensional model separates descriptive attributes from transaction
data, avoids repeating store details on millions of rows, correctly
handles non-sale transaction types, and supports efficient dashboard
slicing by store, category, day of week and month.
